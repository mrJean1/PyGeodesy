Module hom

A pure Python implementation of 2 ellipsoidal and 3 spherical variants of Hotine's Oblique Mercator projection from Snyder Chapter 9<>https://pubs.USGS.gov/pp/1395/report.pdf> page 66ff and IOGP section 3.2.4 page 59ff.

Version: 26.10.18

Classes

Spherical variant 2: Hotine Oblique Mercator projection given the central point and azimuth.

Spherical variant 3: Hotine Oblique Mercator projection given the main pole.

Ellipsoidal variant A: Hotine Oblique Mercator projection given two points.
  HotineObliqueB

8-Tuple (x, y, u, v, lat, lon, scale, hom) with (falsed) east- and northing x and y and "unrectified" u and v in meter, geodetic lat- and longitude in degrees, scale factor (aka k) and HotineOblique* Mercator instance hom.
  HotineAB14Tuple
14-Tuple (A, B, D, D2, E, G, uC, zC, a0, b0, g0, t0, x0, y0) of ellipsoidal map parameters only for variants HotineObliqueA and HotineObliqueB with angles zC, a0, b0 and g0 in radians.
