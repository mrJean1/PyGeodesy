(Constructor)

 

New ellipsoidal LabordeOblique Mercator.

Arguments:
  • lat_latlon - Central latitude (scalar degrees90 or LatLon, 2-tuple (lat, lon) or comma-separated "latDMS, lonDMS" str).
  • lon - Central longitude (degrees180), required if lat_latlon is scalar, ignored otherwise.
  • azimuth - Azimuth of the center line (degrees360 from North).
  • earth - Earth model (Datum, Ellipsoid, Ellipsoid2 or a_f2Tuple).
  • kC - Central scale factor (scalar).
  • x0 - False easting (meter).
  • y0 - False northing (meter).
  • name - Optional name=NN (str).

Project a geodetic lat- and longitude in degrees to Laborde east- and northing.

Arguments:
  • lat_latlon - Latitude of the point (scalar degrees90 or LatLon, 2-tuple (lat, lon) or comma-separated "latDMS, lonDMS" str).

  • name - Optional name=NN (str).

Laborde8Tuple(x, y, U, V, lat, lon, scale, lom) with falsed east- and northing x and y in meter, "unrectified" U and V, geodetic lat- and longitude in degrees, scale factor (aka k) and this LabordeOblique* Mercator instance lom.
